Privacy policy

What Codekin does with your data

Last updated October 15, 2026

Codekin is a game. It has no ads, analytics or tracking. Its online features (live events, code raids, the community challenge and the Code League) use a small server of its own, described under Online play.

The camera

The game uses the camera to find QR codes and to show Codekin, its creatures, in augmented reality. Camera images are processed on your iPhone and never leave it. They aren't saved.

The codes you scan

When you scan a code, the game turns what it says (a web address, Wi-Fi details, a phone number and so on) into a one-way fingerprint on your device and uses that to decide which Codekin lives there. Only the fingerprint is saved, never the code's contents. It isn't sent anywhere except as part of your own iCloud backup and, with online play on, to the Codekin server to check whether a raid boss is there.

Your save

Your progress (trainer name, Codekin, items, coins and which codes you've visited, as fingerprints) is saved on your iPhone. If iCloud backup is on in the game's settings, the save is also stored in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud, so it can be restored on another device. You can turn this off at any time.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game reports achievements and leaderboard scores to Apple's Game Center and shows your friends' standings. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Online play

Live events, code raids, the community challenge and the Code League use Codekin's own server at api.playcodekin.com, hosted by Fly.io. Online play is on unless you turn it off in the game's settings, and the rest of the game works the same without it. With it on, the server keeps:

  • a player ID: your Game Center team player ID if you're signed in to Game Center, or otherwise a random ID made for your iPhone;
  • your Game Center name, which other players see on the league leaderboard and in league battles (the trainer name you type in the game stays on your iPhone);
  • your online play: the raids you've fought in and the damage you did, your league team, battles and rating, and what you've added to community challenges;
  • when you last used it, to the hour, so we can count how many players are active.

To check for raid bosses, the game sends the fingerprint of each code you scan and what kind of code it is, never what the code says, and never your location. To reply, the server handles your IP address, which it doesn't store. This data is used only to run these features. It isn't sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising.

You can delete all of it at any time in the game, under Settings, Online, Delete my online data. That erases your player record and everything linked to it from the server.

Playing with a friend nearby

Face-to-face battles and trades connect directly to the other phone over your local network or Bluetooth, using Apple's peer-to-peer networking. Only what the game needs (your team's Codekin and moves, the Codekin being traded, and your trainer name) is sent, only to that phone, and only while you're playing together.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; the game only learns that a purchase went through so it can add the coins.

Children

Codekin doesn't ask anyone, including children, for personal information. Online play keeps only the game data described above, and it can be deleted from the game at any time.

Support emails

If you email for support, your message and address are used only to reply to you.
